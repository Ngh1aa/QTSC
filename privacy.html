What we collect

When youWe record
Send a form or book a visitName, email, phone, company and your message

Why we use it

To answer you, arrange visits, run campus security and send what you asked for. We do not sell personal data and we do not use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

  • Messages and visit bookings: 12 months.
  • Visitor log: 90 days. CCTV: 30 days.
  • Newsletter: until you unsubscribe.

Who we share it with

Only with the member company you contacted or applied to, and with service providers who work for us under contract.

Your choices

Ask to see, correct or delete your data at privacy@northbank.example. Manage newsletter topics on the newsletter page.
