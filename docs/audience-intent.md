# QTSC Audience, Intent & Top Tasks

## Evidence boundary

This map is derived from the current QTSC route inventory, official QTSC service/policy content, the 2026 anniversary article and the implemented prototype. It is a product hypothesis, not a claim of completed user research. Analytics, CRM enquiries, internal search logs and interviews are still required to validate priority and wording.

## Audience → trigger → task → success

| Priority | Audience in context | Visit trigger | Top task | Confidence evidence | Successful outcome |
|---:|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Technology company / operating team | Needs a location or infrastructure for a new or expanding operation | Compare office, campus, connectivity, Data Center/Cloud and support | Office, telecom, technology and one-stop-service content are first-class routes | Reaches a qualified page, understands fit and starts a contextual enquiry |
| 2 | Investor / strategic partner | Evaluating a technology project, market entry or ecosystem partnership | Understand eligible sectors, incentives, support and collaboration network | QTSC publishes current investment, land and tax guidance plus partnership activity | Identifies eligibility/evidence and contacts the right QTSC owner |
| 3 | Organisation seeking technology | Needs a provider, solution or digital infrastructure | Find a company, solution or QTSC technology service | Directory, Marketplace, DigiTech and Data Center content exist | Finds a shortlist/provider path without browsing the organisation chart |
| 4 | Current member / employee / student | Needs operational information, resources, events or campus utilities | Find a service, document, event, job or place quickly | Resources, amenities, open data, careers and news routes exist | Reaches the required item in one clear path |
| 5 | Talent / media / visitor | Exploring QTSC before a visit, application or story | Understand credibility, activity, location and opportunities | About, Explore, Insights, Media and Careers routes exist | Gains confidence and reaches the relevant detail/handoff |

## Top-task ranking for Home

1. Find an office or operating environment.
2. Evaluate investment and cooperation.
3. Find a technology solution.
4. Find an ecosystem company.
5. Explore the campus.

These are implemented as five high-visibility launchers. They are outcomes, not department names.

## Question / evidence / action matrix

| User question | Required answer | Evidence | Primary action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Is QTSC relevant to my situation? | One-sentence positioning connecting space, digital infrastructure, companies, solutions and investment | 43 ha campus; 25 years; ecosystem scale | Choose a top task |
| Can I trust the scale and capability? | Dated proof, not unqualified marketing claims | QTSC 2026 data and KPMG context | View capability/about source |
| What can I get here? | Four capability paths | Space/infrastructure; technology/security; company/solution; investment/innovation | Open the matching hub |
| What is the place and network like? | Real campus imagery, connected ecosystem and utilities | Official QTSC photography; campus and innovation routes | Explore map, centre, amenity or open data |
| What do I do next? | Contextual route or honest contact handoff | Office, investment and Marketplace decision modules | Continue to detail or connect |

## Homepage implications

- Home is an orientation and routing surface, not a compressed 28-page brochure.
- Critical proof sits after intent, while detailed policy/service content remains on specialised pages.
- News is supporting evidence, not the primary purpose of the first viewport.
- Contact must preserve the selected need and cannot imply server delivery while the prototype has no approved endpoint.
