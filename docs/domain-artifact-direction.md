# QTSC Domain Artifact Direction

## Purpose

Use QTSC's real-world campus and ecosystem as design intelligence, not as decorative skeuomorphism.

Core sequence:

`campus reality → user mental model → structural cue → brand/system adaptation → responsive verification`

Default fidelity: **L1–L2** (cue / structural metaphor).

## Dominant domain grammar

QTSC is not only a generic technology company. It is a **43 ha technology campus and operating ecosystem**. The strongest transferable real-world artifacts are:

1. **Campus map / wayfinding**
   - Use for orientation, explore journeys, building relationships and spatial context.
   - Do not fake a blueprint texture or make the map decorative.

2. **Building directory**
   - Use for office discovery and tenant decision support.
   - Transfer hierarchy: building → location/context → infrastructure/amenities → availability confirmation → enquiry.

3. **Infrastructure topology**
   - Use for telecom, data center, cloud, cybersecurity and service relationships.
   - Prefer clear topology/relationship diagrams over generic network-node decoration.

4. **Company identity / ecosystem directory**
   - Use real company names, verified metadata and real logos when available.
   - Do not invent monograms and present them as brand identity.

5. **Product/service evidence catalogue**
   - Marketplace should prioritize verified provider/product/service information and source links.
   - Representative/mock records must never visually outrank verified QTSC offerings.

## Page application

### Homepage
- Preserve task-first routing and real campus imagery.
- Use network graphics only as a supporting brand signal.
- The campus photo must remain readable as a real place.

### Office
- Primary metaphor: **building directory** (L2 structural).
- Do not imply live availability unless connected to real data.
- Area, rent and conditions remain `UNKNOWN` until confirmed by QTSC.

### Companies
- Primary metaphor: **ecosystem directory** (L1–L2).
- Prefer real identity and location metadata over invented initials.

### Marketplace
- Primary metaphor: **verified capability catalogue** (L2 informational).
- Separate first-party/verified offerings from representative prototype records.

### Investment
- Primary metaphor: **decision dossier / policy register** (L2 structural) when future work changes composition.
- Do not turn policy copy into generic promotional cards.

## Do not copy

- blueprint paper textures;
- fake architectural stamps;
- fake official certificates;
- fake building availability;
- fake company logos;
- fake live infrastructure status;
- decorative network maps that do not help orientation or relationships.

## Evidence baseline — 2026

Use dated source context for material proof points. Current official QTSC public sources support the homepage-level figures used by the prototype, including 43 ha, 90% occupancy, 121 digital technology companies, 22,157 people working/studying and 650 products/solutions/services. Product/service claims must link back to current QTSC first-party sources when used as verified evidence.
