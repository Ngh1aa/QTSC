What we do

Northbank Technology Park builds and runs a campus where technology companies rent offices and labs, use shared infrastructure and find each other. We own the buildings, the data center and the fibre, and we run the services that keep them working.

Members range from startups with six desks in Forge to companies with whole floors in Atlas. Most write software; a growing number design hardware and chips in Delta Labs.

We are a private park operator. Our income comes from rent, data center services and events, and we reinvest it in new buildings and in the campus itself.

How the campus grew

  1. 2009Atlas opensTwelve floors of offices and the first eight member companies.
  2. 2013Beacon opensFurnished suites and coworking for teams of 1 to 30 people.
  3. 2016Cobalt data centerThe first 2 MW hall and two independent fibre routes.
  4. 2019Delta LabsLabs with 3-phase power, shielded rooms and a loading bay.
  5. 2021Echo Hall and GroveA 600-seat conference center, canteen, clinic and gym.
  6. 2023ForgeThe startup center and its first cohort of twelve teams.
  7. 2027Second data center hallOpens in March, doubling rack space in Cobalt.
